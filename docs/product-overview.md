# Product overview

Kiko is a multi-tenant operating workspace for turning business requests into governed work. A
request becomes a command, the Supervisor proposes a plan, specialist agents produce tasks, risky
actions require approval, and every decision remains attributable. Workspaces isolate company data
and apply Owner, Admin, Manager, Member, and Viewer permissions.

The product includes persistent Kiko conversations, commands, projects, a task workbench, approvals,
CRM, marketing campaigns, automations, searchable business knowledge, notifications, analytics,
team administration, integration health, plans/entitlements, and data portability. External systems
are adapters behind explicit configuration, least privilege, approval, idempotency, and audit
controls. Disabled or unverified providers fail closed.

Kiko assists operators; it does not silently publish, email, change source repositories, or execute
high-risk work. AI output is treated as untrusted until validated by schemas and policy. The current
release candidate is intended for authorized staging validation; production activation remains a
separate owner decision.
